Fine print, in normal words

Payment & policies

Because a two-page appeal and a twelve-exhibit appeal aren't the same job. "From" is the floor. After a free consultation you get one fixed number, in writing, before anything starts. It doesn't move afterwards unless you change the scope.
Flat-rate work — resumes, letters — is paid in full up front. Scoped work like appeals and grants is 50% deposit to start, balance on delivery. You get an invoice; no cash-app roulette.
One round within seven days of delivery on every project, two rounds on The Career Comeback. That covers corrections, tone adjustments and swapping details. Rewriting to a completely different brief is a new project, and I'll quote it honestly.
Once writing has started, deposits aren't refundable — that time is already spent. If I can't take your project or can't hit your deadline, I'll say so before you pay, and you'll owe nothing.
No, and be careful of anyone who does. Nobody controls a hiring manager, a review board or a funder. What I guarantee is that your document is the strongest, clearest version of your case that it can be.
On larger projects, yes. Grant writing can be split across milestones. Ask — I've been on a tight budget too, and I'd rather work with you than lose you over timing.
