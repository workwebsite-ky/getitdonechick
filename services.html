Appeals

An appeal is an argument, not a complaint. I read the decision letter, find the standard they're actually judging you against, gather what supports you, and write it as a case — organised, evidenced, and inside your filing window. As the parent of two disabled children, I've written more of these for my own family than I ever planned to.

  • Decision letter reviewed and deadline confirmed first
  • Evidence checklist so nothing goes missing
  • Structured argument, not an emotional letter
  • Submission-ready formatting
