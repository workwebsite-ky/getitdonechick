The woman

Renika Founder & CEO
The story

Corporate didn't fit.
So I built something
that did.

I'm a parent of four. Two of my children are disabled, and the corporate world had no room for what that actually requires. So I stopped asking it to make room. I became a full-time mother and a full-time entrepreneur, and I learned the fastest way there is: by doing it.

A cleaning business. A moving company. Catering. And then teaching other people how to get theirs off the ground. Somewhere in all of that I graduated college with honors — a Bachelor's in Education, then a Master's in Leadership and Business Administration — because I wanted the credentials to match the work.

Now I'm on the road to law school, headed toward entertainment law. Until then, I do what I've always done: I read the fine print, I write the thing that has to be written, and I make sure the people I work with aren't walking into a room unprepared.

Get It Done Chick isn't a slogan. It's a track record.

Mission

Nobody should lose because of how something was worded.

Appeals get denied over structure. Resumes get filtered over formatting. Artists sign away masters because nobody translated clause 14. I close that gap — with professional writing, plain-English explanation, and prices real people can actually pay.

Vision

An advocate in every room where a decision gets made.

Today that's documents and guidance. Tomorrow — law degree in hand — it's representation, with a focus on entertainment and the creators who keep getting outlawyered. Same purpose. Bigger table.

For artists & creators

Breaking down deals
so you don't get played.

The entertainment business runs on paperwork most people sign without reading — and the ones who wrote it are counting on that. Music, film and TV, social media, talent deals, royalties: I break the agreement down until you can explain it back to me in your own words.

That's the whole test. If you can't explain what you signed, you didn't agree to it — you just hoped.

  • Term, territory, exclusivity and rights, explained
  • Royalty splits and recoupment in plain numbers
  • What to negotiate, ranked by what it costs you
